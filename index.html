Privacy Policy

Yellow HRT Companion · Last updated 29 May 2026

Yellow is an HRT (hormone replacement therapy) self-tracking companion made by Adventure Works Solutions LLP ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Yellow HRT Companion mobile app handles, where it goes, and what your rights are.

We've kept it short because the app itself is designed to need very little.

The short version

  • Everything you log in the app — your HRT regimen, symptoms, mood, sleep, period records, and any free-text notes — stays on your phone, inside an encrypted database. We can't see it. Nobody but you can.
  • We don't run analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party SDKs on your health data. None.
  • The only thing that can leave your device is your email address, and only if you choose to create an optional account in a future update. In this TestFlight build, that path is disabled.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

What we collect, and what we don't

On-device only (never transmitted)

The following are stored locally on your iPhone in an encrypted SQLite database (SQLCipher-v4 compatible, AES-256). The encryption key is generated on your device on first launch and stored in the iOS Keychain with the kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly attribute. It is not synced to iCloud. We never see it. We never receive a copy of the database.

  • Your HRT regimen (product, dose, start date, schedule).
  • Daily check-in entries: mood, sleep hours, symptom severities, whether you took your dose.
  • Baseline symptom snapshot from onboarding.
  • Period log entries.
  • Appointment notes and post-appointment outcomes.
  • Any free-text reflections you write.
  • Optional HealthKit data, if you grant permission (read-only, used to display sleep alongside your check-in entries).

Off-device (only if you opt in)

  • Email address — only if you create an account through the optional account screen. This is not part of the default flow and is currently disabled in TestFlight. If/when you do create an account, your email is held by our authentication provider (Supabase) for sign-in purposes only. We don't email you marketing.

Not collected at all

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, address, location, IP-based identifiers (IDFA, IDFV), device identifiers, contacts, browsing history, search history, or usage data.
  • We do not embed any advertising or analytics SDK.
  • We do not run a crash reporter in the current build.

HealthKit

If you grant HealthKit access, Yellow reads sleep data from Apple Health so it can show it alongside your daily check-in. This data is read on-device only and is never transmitted off your phone. We do not write any data back to HealthKit in the current build.

Per Apple's rules, HealthKit data is never used for advertising, marketing, or shared with third parties — and never will be.

Future cloud sync (not active in TestFlight)

Yellow is being built so that, in a future release, you can optionally sync your data across your own devices. If and when that feature ships, the sync will be end-to-end encrypted on your device before upload. Our servers will only ever see ciphertext. We will update this policy before that feature is enabled.

Children

Yellow is not directed at children under 16. HRT is a treatment used in adults, and the app is designed for adult use only.

Your rights (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

Because almost nothing leaves your device, most data-subject rights are exercised directly in the app:

  • Access / portability: all your data is on your device. A data export feature is on the roadmap; in the interim, contact us and we'll walk you through it.
  • Erasure: uninstalling Yellow removes the encrypted database and its key from your device. If you created an optional account, email us to delete the email + UUID server-side.
  • Rectification: edit any entry directly in the app.
  • Objection / restriction: disable any feature in Settings, or uninstall.

We are the data controller for the optional account data. For UK residents, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Security

  • Local database: AES-256 via SQLite3MultipleCiphers, SQLCipher-v4 compatible mode.
  • Key storage: iOS Keychain, device-only, first-unlock accessibility.
  • No plaintext copy of your health data is written anywhere on disk outside the encrypted database.
  • Source code is reviewed against the OWASP Mobile Top 10.

What Yellow is not

Yellow is a self-tracking companion. It is not a medical device, a diagnostic tool, or a substitute for advice from your GP, menopause specialist, or other regulated healthcare professional. The in-app copy is static and clinically reviewed; it does not generate medical advice using AI, and it does not recommend doses.

If you have an urgent medical concern, contact your healthcare provider or your local emergency service.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change (for example, when cloud sync ships), we'll update the "Last updated" date and notify you in-app before the change takes effect.
